### 4. What is the primary reason carbon atoms form up to four covalent bonds?

- [ ] To become positively charged
- [ ] To satisfy the octet rule
- [ ] To increase its atomic number
- [ ] To become a noble gas

<details>
<summary>View Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Correct Answer:** To satisfy the octet rule

*Explanation: The document explains that carbon atoms form up to four covalent bonds 'to satisfy the octet rule' because they have an incomplete outermost electron shell.*

</details>
